Working with TensorBlack.

Sponsorship, faculty nominations, speaking, and press. Four routes in, one inbox, and a line that commercial support does not cross.

Ways in

Four routes, one inbox

Sponsorship and partnership

For organizations that want to reach the clinicians and administrators making AI decisions in cancer care. Support underwrites the production of independent programming across the podcast, the newsletter, the Academy, and SHIFT. Every placement is labeled.

What to send

The audience you are trying to reach, and the quarter you are planning for.

Faculty nomination

Faculty membership is by invitation, and most invitations begin with a colleague's recommendation. Faculty work: they record media, lead sessions at the CEO Round Table, teach Academy cohorts, and speak at our conferences. We do not accept applications.

What to send

Who you are recommending, their specialty, and what you think they would take on.

Speaking and program participation

SHIFT convenes clinical, operational, and policy leaders around the decisions in front of them this year. The CEO Round Table is a closed session where cancer program executives share what they are deploying and what they are learning from it.

What to send

The session you have in mind, and the decision it puts in front of the room.

Press and media

For journalists covering oncology, health systems, and clinical AI. We can point you to faculty who work in the area you are writing about, and we say so when a subject has a relevant financial relationship.

What to send

Your deadline, and what you need.

The line

What commercial support does and does not include

Support pays for the production of independent programming. It does not buy influence over it. The boundary is the same one that governs everything we publish.

Support includes

  • Underwriting the production of programming
  • Labeled placements across content, programs, and events
  • Presence and participation at SHIFT
  • Reach into an audience of oncology decision-makers

Support does not include

  • Selecting topics
  • Reviewing content prior to publication
  • Approving faculty
  • Appearing as editorial rather than as sponsorship

Disclosure

What we disclose, and who runs what

Faculty disclose financial relationships relevant to the subject matter, and those disclosures are published alongside the content. Sponsored placements are labeled as such wherever they appear.

Conference and event operations are run through Loran Media Group. Advisory relationships, where they exist, are separated from editorial and disclosed.

We are not a consultancy and we do not resell software. If what you need is implementation work, we are the wrong partner, and we will say so early.

What happens next

What happens after you write

  1. 01

    It arrives in one place

    Sponsorship, nominations, speaking, and press all reach the same inbox. You do not have to work out who owns your question.

  2. 02

    It goes to whoever owns it

    Sponsorship goes to the commercial team, faculty nominations to the faculty lead, and press to the editorial side. The routing happens on our end.

  3. 03

    Sponsorship starts with the calendar

    The first conversation is about what is being produced and when, because the production schedule determines what is available.
