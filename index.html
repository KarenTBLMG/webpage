Who we are

A physician-led media and education company built for cancer care

TensorBlack was founded by practicing oncologists and health system operators who were being asked the same questions from every direction: which of these AI tools is real, and what happens to my patients and my margins if I get it wrong?

We answer those questions in public. We publish, we convene, and we teach — through a weekly podcast and newsletter, an executive roundtable, a national summit, and a course library. Everything we make is produced with clinicians and administrators who carry the decision themselves.

We are not a consultancy and we do not resell software. Our work is editorial, educational, and convening, and it is built with our faculty coalition of national oncology leaders, spanning the specialties where cancer is actually treated.

How we work

Independence, disclosure, and who pays for what

Editorial and educational content is developed by TensorBlack faculty and staff. Sponsors and commercial partners do not select topics, review content prior to publication, or approve faculty.

Faculty disclose financial relationships relevant to the subject matter, and those disclosures are published alongside the content. Sponsored placements are labeled as such wherever they appear.

Conference and event operations are run through Loran Media Group. Advisory relationships, where they exist, are separated from editorial and disclosed.
