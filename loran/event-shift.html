SHIFT: OncologyInnovation Summit

About a hundred chief medical officers, cancer-center executives, founders and investors — in one room, for two days, on what AI in cancer care actually takes.

When
September 2026
Where
The Thompson, Austin
Room
~100, by invitation
Status
Completed

On the table

Four questions every cancer program is facing now.

Chosen by the providers in the room, not the sponsors funding it. Findings from the summit will be published this fall.

01

Clinical validation

What evidence should a cancer program require before an AI tool touches a patient decision?

02

Operational readiness

Workflow, staffing and data — what has to be true inside the building before deployment works.

03

Reimbursement

Who pays for AI in oncology care, and how programs make the case today.

04

Governance

Oversight, accountability and trust once the tools are live.

Who was in the room

Providers first. Everyone else, a guest.

Co-convened

TensorBlack builds the understanding. LORAN builds the room.

TensorBlack is an always-on education and consulting platform for AI in oncology. LORAN is the small, vetted, in-person room. Together, a year of learning turns into two days where decisions actually get made.
