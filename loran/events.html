How a seat is filled

Curation is the product.

Not the venue, not the agenda, not the badge. Here’s what happens between “request a seat” and the room.

  1. Request or nomination

    You ask, or a peer puts your name forward.

  2. Vetting

    We confirm role, practice and fit for this room.

  3. Balance

    Providers first; every other seat is weighed against the mix.

  4. Invitation

    A personal invitation and registration link.

  5. The room

    Off the record, and in touch all year.
