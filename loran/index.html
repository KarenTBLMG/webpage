Three formats, one standard

Every room is curated. Every seat is earned.

Oncology is where we proved the model. Other specialties join as AI reaches the same inflection point — the standard for who’s in the room never changes.

Why providers trust the room

Trust is bought by who’s in the room — and how tightly it’s curated.

  1. A provider-led agenda

    Clinicians, not sponsors, choose the topics. The questions on the program are the ones keeping practicing providers up at night.

  2. Vetted, capped rooms

    Every attendee is a peer, transparently curated. When the room is full, it’s full — no matter who is asking.

  3. A bias firewall

    Sponsors get access and relationships, never editorial control. We say so out loud, and we put it in writing.

  4. Clinically useful

    CME-grade rigor, real cases, honest debate. You should leave with something you can act on Monday.

  5. Continuity

    A year-round community, not a one-night stand. Regional forums and virtual sessions keep the conversation going between summits.
