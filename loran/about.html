About LORAN

We’re in the businessof trusted peer counsel.

Delivered through curated, live convening. For LORAN, the event isn’t just the format. It’s the product.

What we do

The independent room, crafted for quality over scale.

LORAN hosts invitation-only summits, regional forums and virtual summits for senior healthcare leaders at the intersection of clinical care and technology.

Our job is the room itself: sourcing, vetting and hosting the people who need to be together, and designing the hours they spend there so trust and decisions happen faster than anywhere else.

We’re built for practicing providers first — physicians, nurses and navigators, pharmacists — and the health-system leaders who set the tone. Industry, investors and technology companies are guests in the providers’ room, never the reason it exists.

Oncology is where we prove the model first, not the ceiling on what it can do. Other specialties join as AI reaches the same inflection point in their care.

What makes the room different

No one else delivers all four at once.

01

Independent & neutral

We’re not owned by a society, a membership network or a media conglomerate. No one else’s agenda comes with the invitation.

02

Deliberately small & vetted

Others run 750 to 2,300 and up. We seat about a hundred, and we know why each of them is there.

03

One peer room, provider-first

Practicing clinicians, nurses and navigators at the same table as health-system leadership, investors and AI builders.

04

Curation as the product

“We decide who’s in the room” is the promise. Not the venue, not the agenda, not the badge.

How we host

The smartest, calmest colleague in the room.

Credible, candid, discerning, warm, unhurried. That’s the host we try to be.

  1. We decide who belongs

    And we set the conditions for real conversation, rather than explaining the landscape from a stage.

  2. We respect your time

    Plain, precise, peer-to-peer. Every hour in the room is designed to be worth leaving the clinic for.

  3. We keep it off the record

    Honesty needs a room small enough, and private enough, to be honest in.

  4. We stay in touch

    A year-round community between the summits, not a one-night event.

About the name

LORAN was the long-range navigation system ships and aircraft relied on before GPS — steady, trusted guidance through uncertain waters. We kept the name because the instinct still fits: in a field moving this fast, the most reliable signal is the right people in the same room.
