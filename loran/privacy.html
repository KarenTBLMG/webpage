Who we are

This website is operated by LORAN Media Group, LLC (“LORAN”, “we”, “us”).

What we collect

When you submit a form, we receive the details you enter: your name, work email, title, organization and any message. When you subscribe to Field Notes, we receive your email address. We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand how the site is used in aggregate.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request or inquiry
  • To evaluate invitations and nominations for our events
  • To send Field Notes, if you subscribe

What we don’t do

We do not sell your information, and we do not share attendee or contact details with sponsors for marketing.

Where it goes

Form submissions are delivered by email to our team through a transactional email provider. The website itself does not store your submissions. Event registration is handled by a third-party platform with its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or unsubscribe at any time, by contacting us through the contact page.
