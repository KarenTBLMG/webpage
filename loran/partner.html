Why it works

The firewall is the feature.

Senior providers come because nobody is selling to them from the stage. That trust is exactly what gives partners real relationships with the people who make decisions.

What partners get

  • Time with senior healthcare decision-makers, in a room of about a hundred
  • Seats at the table as guests, alongside providers, health-system leaders and investors
  • Relationships that continue through regional forums and virtual sessions
  • Clear, written terms for every engagement, before it begins
  • Recognition as a partner, stated openly to every attendee

What partners never get

  • Control over the topics, the program or the speakers
  • Paid placement presented as education
  • Attendee lists for outbound marketing
  • A say in who is invited
  • Anything we wouldn’t tell the room about
