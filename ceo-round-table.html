Build for oncology with oncology in the room.

The CEO Round Table gives AI and technology companies direct guidance from oncology leaders on policy, implementation, and positioning — while the decisions are still open.

What it is

A closed session with the people who would use what you are building

Technology teams bring a product, a roadmap, or an open question. Oncology leaders respond to it directly: what works, what would stall, and what the people who would actually use it need from it.

The value is timing. Most teams hear this after launch, from a customer. The Round Table puts it in front of you while the design is still yours to change.

What we work on

Policy, implementation, and positioning

Policy

Where regulation, reimbursement, and health system policy are heading, and what that means for what you are building. Our policy faculty work in government affairs, regulatory strategy, payer and value-based care, and reimbursement.

Implementation

What it takes to deploy inside a cancer program: the workflows it touches, the staff who would run it, the data it depends on, and the operational reality that decides whether a pilot becomes standard practice.

Positioning

How your product reads to the clinicians and executives who would buy and use it, which claims land, and which ones a clinical audience will not accept.

Who is in the room

Oncology leaders, not a panel of generalists

The room is drawn from our faculty: national leaders across medical, radiation, and surgical oncology, pharmacy and nursing operations, policy and regulation, and clinical informatics. They are the people running cancer programs, not commentators on them.
