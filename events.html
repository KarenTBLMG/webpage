The decisions in front of oncology this year.

SHIFT is our national summit on driving innovation in oncology care, convening clinical, operational, and policy leaders around the decisions in front of them now rather than the ones five years out.

The summit

SHIFT

One programme, built around what cancer programs are deciding this year: which tools are real, what it takes to deploy them, and what changes for the people delivering care.

Sessions are led by faculty who run cancer programs, and the room is built to match — clinical, operational, policy, and technology leaders in the same place on the same question.

The audience

Who is in the room

Oncologists, cancer program executives, pharmacy and nursing leaders, and the policy specialists who work alongside them.
